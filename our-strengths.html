LOGISTICS CORRIDOR

The Gujarat Port Advantage

Operating from Ahmedabad places Kanha Global Exports at the epicenter of India’s most efficient maritime export corridor.

Mundra Port India's largest commercial deep-draft container gateway with direct vessel departures.
JNPT / Nhava Sheva Direct highway corridor to Mumbai's premier container transshipment port.
Pipavav & Hazira Strategic secondary container and bulk marine terminals for rapid port turnaround.
Faster Sea Transit Shorter sailing times to Middle East (GCC), Europe, Africa, North America, and Southeast Asia.
Direct Mandi Hub Immediate proximity to Asia's largest spice and oilseed market yards in Unjha & Gondal.
Cold Chain Reefer Equipped for fresh produce and frozen seafood reefer container stuffing under optimal temp control.
