ABOUT KANHA GLOBAL EXPORTS

Connecting Indian Supply with Global Demand

Established in 2022 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, Kanha Global Exports is a merchant export company serving international buyers across food and agricultural product categories.

Our portfolio includes spices, basmati and non-basmati rice, pulses and lentils, agricultural commodities, fresh produce, and seafood.

Our objective is straightforward:
Reliable sourcing. Consistent communication. Quality-focused execution. Long-term partnerships.

We work with a network of suppliers, processors and logistics partners to connect Indian products with international markets.
