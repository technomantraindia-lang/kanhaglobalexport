CORE EXPORT PHILOSOPHY

"We do not believe that export quality is only about appearance."

True export excellence is a multi-dimensional discipline delivered across 6 interconnected pillars on every global consignment:

01

Specification

Precise alignment on grain length, moisture limits, purity levels, mesh sizing, and chemical parameters before procurement.

02

Safety

Rigorous statutory compliance with pesticide residue limits, aflatoxin thresholds, microbial safety, and heavy metals testing.

03

Consistency

Standardized lot-by-lot grading and uniform moisture content ensuring every container mirrors the approved reference sample.

04

Documentation

Comprehensive export paperwork including Phytosanitary, Certificate of Origin, COA laboratory reports, and clear Bill of Lading.

05

Traceability

Complete supply chain transparency with APMC mandi source lotting, processing batch records, and verifiable origin provenance.

06

Delivery Support

Shipment tracking and documentation updates.
