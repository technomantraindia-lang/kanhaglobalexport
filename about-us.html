METHODOLOGY & SOURCING PIPELINE

Our Sourcing Approach

We work with selected suppliers, processors and manufacturers based on product requirements, commercial suitability and buyer specifications.

01

Supplier Selection

Direct origin sourcing with audited farmers, certified millers, and verified processors.

02

Product Specification

Precise calibration for moisture, grain length, purity, mesh size, and buyer parameters.

03

Quality Checks

Multi-tier inspection, NABL lab testing, COA verification, and safety compliance.

04

Packing & Labeling

Custom food-grade retail pouches, multi-wall sacks, bulk jumbo bags, and barcoding.

05

Documentation

Phytosanitary, Certificate of Origin, Bill of Lading, and customs export filing.

06

Shipment & Delivery

Container stuffing at Mundra/JNPT ports with real-time freight and vessel tracking.
