WHO WE ARE

Connecting Indian Supply with Global Demand

Kanha Global Exports operates as a dedicated merchant export house based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, Western India.

Kanha Global Exports was established in 2022 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India as an India-based merchant export company dedicated to supplying premium Indian food and agricultural products to international markets.

We connect international importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers and private-label businesses with reliable sourcing opportunities from India's rich agricultural hubs.

Our Objective: Reliable sourcing. Consistent communication. Quality-focused execution. Long-term partnerships.

We work with a verified network of suppliers, processors, and logistics partners to connect Indian agricultural capabilities with international destination requirements seamlessly.
