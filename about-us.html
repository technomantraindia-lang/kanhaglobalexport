Who We Are

Built on Trust, Driven by Excellence

Headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, Kanha Global Exports combines generational commodity wisdom with state-of-the-art export compliance.

Kanha Global Exports was founded in 2022 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat (Western India) by two dynamic brothers, Mr. Piyush Patel and Mr. Jaymin Patel, driven by a vision to bring the finest Indian agricultural commodities, animal feed supplements, Basmati rice, spices, fruits, and vegetables to global markets.

Established and backed by a 3rd generation business house, we believe that enduring business grows on Faith & Trust, along with Commitment & Passion. Starting with precision small-scale exports focused on strict quality and punctual delivery, we rapidly scaled into a full-fledged international merchant export house serving buyers worldwide.

"Partners in Progress": As merchant exporters, we collaborate closely with verified Indian manufacturers and farm collectives — helping them market their premium products globally while giving international buyers a dependable, single-window procurement gateway.

Planet at Par with Profits: We have evolved a responsible code of conduct that provides the foundation for the way we do business, ensuring sustainable procurement practices and environmental protection across our entire supply chain.
